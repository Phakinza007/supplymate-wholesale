# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thai restaurant, café, and central-kitchen buyers who need to browse wholesale food-service supplies with clear units, pack sizes, and minimum order quantities.

## Product Purpose

SupplyMate is a static, local-data-only concept wholesale catalogue. It demonstrates a credible B2B browsing and simulated checkout flow while stating clearly that it cannot receive or store real orders.

## Brand Personality

Dependable, operational, and candid. The experience should feel practical and trustworthy for professional buyers without implying services or capabilities that the demo does not provide.

## Anti-references

- Discount-marketplace or consumer-retail treatment
- Gradients and promotional visual noise
- Fake stock counts, shipping promises, reviews, or real-order claims
- Any interaction or copy that obscures the concept-demo boundary

## Design Principles

1. Put wholesale facts before promotional language.
2. Make the professional buyer context clear at first glance.
3. Keep every demo limitation visible and candid.
4. Use restrained, operational presentation that supports fast comparison.
5. Preserve familiar catalogue and cart interactions without inventing unsupported behaviour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic labels, visible keyboard focus, sufficient colour contrast, and reduced-motion alternatives. Maintain a layout without horizontal overflow at 375 × 812 pixels and keep all essential information available without hover.
